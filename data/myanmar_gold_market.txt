MYANMAR GOLD MARKET REGULATIONS AND TRENDS 2024

1. Gold Trading Framework
The Myanmar gold market operates under dual systems:
- Official Market: Regulated by Yangon Gold Entrepreneurs Association (YGEA), sets daily reference price
- Informal Market: Parallel trading at premium rates, estimated 20-30% of total market volume

2. Gold Price Determination
YGEA publishes daily reference prices based on:
- International spot gold price (London Bullion Market)
- USD/MMK exchange rate (market rate)
- Local demand and supply factors
- Typical premium: 5-10% above international spot price

3. Gold Trading Regulations
- Licensed gold shops must register with YGEA
- All transactions above 1 tical (16.33 grams) must be recorded
- Digital payment encouraged to create transaction trail
- Cash transactions above MMK 5,000,000 require customer identification

4. Gold Import/Export Rules
- Gold imports require CBM license and payment in foreign currency
- Export of gold jewelry allowed with proper documentation
- Raw gold export restricted to licensed refineries
- Import duty: 5% on gold bars, 10% on gold jewelry

5. Investment Gold Products
Banks offer gold savings products:
- KBZ Bank: Gold Savings Account (minimum 0.5 tical)
- CB Bank: Gold Investment Plan
- AYA Bank: Gold Certificate Program
- Returns linked to international gold price movements

6. Market Size and Trends
- Estimated annual gold trading volume: 50-70 tonnes
- Peak trading seasons: Thadingyut (October), Tazaungdaing (November), Thingyan (April)
- Average daily trading: 200-300 kg in Yangon market
- Main trading centers: Yangon (Bogyoke Market, Shwe Bon Thar Street), Mandalay

7. Gold as Inflation Hedge
Myanmar households traditionally hold gold as:
- Inflation protection (especially during currency depreciation)
- Emergency savings (liquidity during crises)
- Wedding and religious ceremony requirements
- Estimated 60-70% of households hold some gold assets

8. Price Volatility Factors
Gold prices in Myanmar fluctuate due to:
- International gold spot prices (primary driver)
- MMK exchange rate movements
- Local demand spikes (festivals, political uncertainty)
- Supply constraints (import restrictions)
- Seasonal patterns (higher demand during festival months)

9. Digital Gold Trading
Emerging platforms:
- KBZPay Gold: Digital gold purchase/sale through mobile app
- Wave Money: Gold-backed savings products
- Challenges: Trust in digital gold vs physical possession preference

10. Regulatory Compliance
Gold shops must comply with:
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations
- Tax reporting (income tax on trading profits)
- Consumer protection (accurate weighing, quality certification)
- YGEA membership and code of conduct
