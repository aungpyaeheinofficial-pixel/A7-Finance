MYANMAR TAX REGULATIONS AND COMPLIANCE 2024

1. Corporate Income Tax
- Standard rate: 25% of taxable income
- Small businesses (annual revenue < MMK 50 million): 3% flat rate
- Special Economic Zones (SEZ): 5-7% for first 5 years, then 15%
- Tax year: April 1 to March 31
- Filing deadline: June 30 (3 months after year-end)
- Advance tax: 4 quarterly installments based on previous year's tax

2. Personal Income Tax
Tax brackets for individuals:
- 0-2,000,000 MMK: 0%
- 2,000,001-5,000,000 MMK: 5%
- 5,000,001-10,000,000 MMK: 10%
- 10,000,001-20,000,000 MMK: 15%
- 20,000,001-30,000,000 MMK: 20%
- Above 30,000,000 MMK: 25%
- Annual filing deadline: March 31

3. Commercial Tax (Sales Tax)
- Standard rate: 5% on goods and services
- Exempt items: Basic food items, medicine, educational materials
- Reduced rate: 0% for exports
- Special rates: 8% for luxury goods, 10% for alcohol and tobacco
- Filing: Monthly returns due by 15th of following month

4. Withholding Tax
Applicable rates:
- Interest income: 15%
- Dividend income: 10%
- Royalty income: 15%
- Service fees: 2.5% (for residents), 2.5-15% (for non-residents)
- Rent income: 10%
- Professional services: 10%

5. Capital Gains Tax
- Real estate: 10% on gains from property sales
- Securities: 10% on stock trading profits
- Exemptions: Principal residence (one-time), assets held > 3 years (reduced rate)

6. Value Added Tax (VAT)
- Not currently implemented in Myanmar
- Under consideration for future tax reform
- Commercial Tax serves similar function

7. Customs and Import Duties
- General import duty: 0-40% depending on product category
- Essential goods (medicine, food): 0-5%
- Luxury goods: 15-40%
- Export duty: Generally 0%, except for raw materials (5-10%)
- Special Economic Zones: Reduced or zero duties

8. Stamp Duty
- Property transactions: 2-4% of property value
- Loan agreements: 0.1% of loan amount
- Share transfers: 0.5% of transaction value
- Lease agreements: 0.5% of annual rent

9. Special Business Tax (SBT)
Applies to specific sectors:
- Hotels and tourism: 5% of revenue
- Restaurants: 3% of revenue
- Entertainment: 10% of revenue
- Telecommunications: 5% of revenue

10. Tax Incentives
Available for:
- Manufacturing in SEZs: 5-7 year tax holiday
- Export-oriented businesses: Reduced rates
- Agriculture and livestock: Tax exemptions
- Education and healthcare: Tax benefits
- Infrastructure projects: Special concessions

11. Tax Compliance Requirements
All businesses must:
- Register with Internal Revenue Department (IRD)
- Obtain Tax Identification Number (TIN)
- Maintain proper accounting records (English or Myanmar)
- File monthly Commercial Tax returns
- File annual Income Tax returns
- Keep records for 6 years minimum

12. Penalties and Interest
- Late filing: 10% penalty on tax due
- Late payment: 2% per month interest
- Underpayment: 50% penalty on tax shortfall
- Non-compliance: Additional penalties and possible prosecution

13. Double Taxation Treaties
Myanmar has DTA with:
- Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, India, South Korea, UK
- Reduces withholding tax rates for cross-border transactions
- Provides tax credit mechanisms

14. Transfer Pricing
- Related party transactions must be at arm's length
- Documentation required for transactions > MMK 50 million
- CBM and IRD monitor transfer pricing compliance
