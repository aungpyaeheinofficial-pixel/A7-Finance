MYANMAR BANKING REGULATIONS AND POLICIES 2024

1. Central Bank of Myanmar (CBM) Monetary Policy
The CBM maintains a managed float exchange rate system. The reference rate is published daily at 9:00 AM Myanmar Standard Time. Commercial banks are required to trade within a 0.5% band of the reference rate for official transactions.

2. Foreign Currency Account Regulations
- Resident individuals can hold foreign currency accounts (FCA) with authorized banks.
- Minimum deposit: USD 1,000 or equivalent.
- Withdrawals require documentation proving legitimate business or travel purposes.
- Non-residents have unrestricted FCA access.

3. Remittance Services
International remittances through licensed money transfer operators (MTOs) are capped at:
- USD 10,000 per transaction for individuals
- USD 50,000 per transaction for businesses
- All remittances must be reported to the CBM within 24 hours.

4. Bank Licensing Requirements
New commercial bank licenses require:
- Minimum paid-up capital: MMK 20 billion
- At least 3 years of financial services experience
- Compliance with Basel III capital adequacy ratios (minimum 8% Tier 1 capital)

5. Digital Payment Systems
The CBM has approved the following mobile payment platforms:
- KBZPay (Kanbawza Bank)
- Wave Money (Telenor Myanmar)
- CB Pay (CB Bank)
- AYA Pay (AYA Bank)
- UAB Pay (United Amara Bank)

All platforms must maintain transaction records for 7 years and report suspicious activities to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).

6. Interest Rate Policy
As of 2024, the CBM policy rate stands at 8.5%. Commercial banks are free to set deposit and lending rates, but must maintain a minimum spread of 2% between deposit and lending rates to ensure profitability.

7. Credit Card Regulations
- Credit cards issued in Myanmar can only be used domestically.
- International credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) are accepted at hotels and select merchants but subject to 2% foreign transaction fee.
- Maximum credit limit: MMK 5,000,000 per cardholder.

8. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Requirements
All financial institutions must:
- Conduct customer due diligence (CDD) for all accounts
- Report transactions exceeding MMK 10,000,000 to the FIU
- Maintain records for 5 years minimum
- Train staff on AML compliance annually
