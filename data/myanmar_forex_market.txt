MYANMAR FOREIGN EXCHANGE MARKET ANALYSIS 2024

1. Exchange Rate Mechanisms
The Myanmar Kyat (MMK) exchange rate is determined through a dual-track system:
- Official Rate: Set by CBM daily, used for government transactions and priority imports
- Market Rate: Determined by supply and demand, typically 10-15% higher than official rate
- Parallel Market: Informal trading occurs at rates 20-30% above official rate

2. Major Currency Pairs
As of Q2 2024, average exchange rates:
- USD/MMK Official: 2,100 MMK per USD
- USD/MMK Market: 2,350-2,450 MMK per USD
- EUR/MMK: 2,550-2,650 MMK per EUR
- THB/MMK: 65-70 MMK per THB
- CNY/MMK: 320-330 MMK per CNY

3. Export Earnings Conversion
Exporters must convert foreign currency earnings as follows:
- 35% at official CBM rate (mandatory within 1 business day)
- 65% can be retained or sold at market rates
- Export proceeds must be received through authorized dealer banks
- Documentation required: Export license, shipping documents, invoice

4. Import Payment Regulations
Importers face restrictions based on priority:
- Priority Imports (fuel, medicine, fertilizer): Can access official rate through banks
- General Imports: Must purchase foreign currency at market rates
- Luxury Goods: Import ban remains in effect
- Payment terms: Maximum 90 days credit for imports

5. Foreign Investment Currency Rules
Foreign direct investment (FDI) companies:
- Can repatriate profits after tax payment
- Must convert 50% of local revenue to foreign currency through authorized banks
- Capital repatriation requires CBM approval
- Minimum investment: USD 50,000 for manufacturing, USD 300,000 for services

6. Remittance and Transfer Fees
- Outward remittances: 0.5% fee + telegraphic transfer charges
- Inward remittances: No fees for recipients
- Maximum transfer: USD 10,000 per day for individuals
- Business transfers: Up to USD 50,000 with proper documentation

7. Currency Exchange Points
Authorized currency exchange counters:
- Must be licensed by CBM
- Can only buy/sell major currencies (USD, EUR, SGD, THB, CNY)
- Daily transaction limit: MMK 10,000,000 per customer
- Must report transactions exceeding MMK 5,000,000

8. Market Volatility Factors
Key factors affecting MMK exchange rate:
- Political stability and security situation
- Trade balance (exports vs imports)
- Foreign reserves level (estimated USD 1.2 billion as of 2024)
- Remittance inflows (estimated USD 2.5 billion annually)
- Regional economic conditions (especially Thailand and China)
